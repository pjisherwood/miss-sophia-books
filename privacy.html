Privacy notice

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Who I am

Miss Sophia Books is run by Paul Isherwood, a sole trader in England. For anything about your details, write to misssophiabooks@gmail.com.

What I keep when you buy something

  • Your email address, and your name and billing country if the payment page asks for them.
  • What you bought and when, and the boxes you ticked on my checkout page (that you agreed to instant access, and whether you want occasional news), with the date and wording.
  • Your payment. You pay on Stripe’s secure page. Your card details go to Stripe and never reach me or this website.

These records are held in my Stripe account, which is where I look them up.

What is kept on your phone

After you buy, a private key (a long code) and the place you got to in each story are kept in your phone’s browser so your stories open without a password. I do not use advertising or tracking cookies on the shop pages.

Why I use your details

  • To take your payment, give you your audiobook, and email you your private link (so I can carry out the sale).
  • To answer you if you write to me, and to deal with refunds or problems.
  • To keep sales records, which the law requires for tax purposes (I keep these for the time the law asks).
  • To send occasional news of new stories, only if you ticked the box. You can change your mind at any time by emailing me. I am not sending any news emails yet.

Who else handles your details

  • Stripe, to take payments. For some sales outside the UK, Stripe (selling through its Link service) is the seller, and its own privacy notice applies to that sale.
  • Resend, to send you your private link email.
  • Cloudflare, which stores the private audio files (no personal details are stored with them).
  • Netlify, which hosts the website.
  • ElevenLabs, which runs the chat with Miss Sophia on the home and Ask Miss Sophia pages if you choose to use it.
  • Google Fonts, which supplies the lettering on the pages, so your device’s address is sent to Google when a page loads.

Some of these companies are based outside the UK. I only use well-known services that protect personal data. I do not sell your details.

Your rights

You can ask to see what I hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted (apart from records I must keep by law). Just email me. If you are unhappy with how I have handled your details, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.
